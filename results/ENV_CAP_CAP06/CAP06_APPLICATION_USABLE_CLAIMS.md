# Claims usable in a research application

1. NUM03 provides accepted, conditional first-return ray geometry in one WOA climate-source family. CAP06 does not turn it into a full-wave or detectability certificate.
2. CAP06 implements explicit source/gain nuisance controls on finite complex receiver samples. Arbitrary per-epoch differential gain removes relative-power information in the registered mathematical model. Weak-energy background subtraction can make power ratios undefined; these cases remain missing rather than being silently clipped.
3. CAP06 establishes a reproducible nonoracle array-front-end benchmark with separate known-direction calibration, left/right mirror candidates, missing sources, distractors, navigation and timing assumptions. Its achieved numerical outcomes are in the sealed P2 tables; any positive result is conditional on the plane-wave receiver model.
4. The scientific question is how extractable propagation information survives unknown source, calibration, environment and geometric branch ambiguity, and what additional resources are necessary. Power normalization, ordinary beamforming and larger baselines alone are not claimed as new algorithms.
5. Candidate support, finite-estimator error and engineering accuracy are separate. The resource table can support a bounded research proposal and explicit failure boundaries, without promising five-parameter precision or globally minimal equipment.

Use each claim together with its EVIDENCE_LEVEL_MATRIX.csv source and blocker. No procurement, sea-trial or partner endorsement is implied.
