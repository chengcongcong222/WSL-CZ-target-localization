For x(t)=A exp{i[2pi(F0-F_LO)-kq]t}, receive-center derotation removes both unknown source offset and constant motion shift. The residual may be constant even with nonzero q. This is a single-component counterexample about this processor; it is not a no-go theorem for multimodal methods. Restoring a receive center alone cannot identify F0 and q separately.

Known public LO is a receiver coordinate, not an emitted-frequency reference. Receive-estimated center is an allowed mixture of source and propagation. Private source frequency/phase, true trajectory/depth and REF output are forbidden to the new receiver processor. A sanitized public column/index copy is its only metadata input. Old residual outputs are preserved for comparison, never used to select a new peak.

A digital LO change delta maps x to x exp(-i2pi delta t), LO to LO+delta. Transforming back before the same physical beam/filter restores the same receive frequency. This invariance does not identify an unknown source.

Candidate relation F_rx,j approximately F0(1-qbar_j/c) gives Delta F_rx approximately -(F0/c)Delta qbar. The receiver uses its own mean frequency for a diagnostic scale only. Unknown phase speed, multimodal weighting and finite-window weighting remain limitations. For drifting F0 the difference includes Delta F0 and a changed motion product, so stable-source cancellation does not extend to DRIFT.
