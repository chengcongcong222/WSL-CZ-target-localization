Park Eq1 uses p(f+Δ/2)p*(f−Δ/2). Eq3–6 frequency-average the AP vector, form its normalized CSDM, match with a replica, and average difference-frequency outputs. A common unknown source spectrum does not in general factor out of bandwidth averaging across different pairs. Our **normalize each pair first** construction removes its common scalar exactly and keeps the inter-pair noise covariance. The processors therefore differ. In the original Park FD-MFP, the replica is a difference-frequency acoustic field; that substitution is **not** used here. No equivalence AP=H(Δ) is asserted.

Each original PDF/XML/HTML is archived with raw/source hashes in the freeze. Acquisition failures are preserved. FULL means recovered complete document, not all reported experimental parameters were disclosed.
