# Primary formula audit notes (no change to frozen custom processor)

Park2024 Eq1 is the same elementwise high-frequency conjugate product used here. Its Eq3–6 first average the AP vector over center frequency, then normalize and match a replica; this screen normalizes each individual pair and propagates joint noise instead. These operations need not commute under unknown source spectrum. The publisher MathML Eq5 denominator repeats the conjugate-transposed AP expression, an apparent dimensional printing conflict. No strict reconstruction silently repairs it. Alongside the printed frequency-spacing/count conflict and absent aperture/count, this limits exact original-processor reproduction; it does not alter the explicitly defined custom CA.

Yin2026 Eq4–5 form/average normalized AP outer products; Eq9–11 instead average high-frequency replica autoproduct vectors and normalize the resulting replica. Eq12 combines difference-frequency scores noncoherently. The mismatch between true acoustic H(Delta) and the high-frequency autoproduct is precisely why its FDSL replica also needs the high-frequency pair. This screen retains all cross-mode products and never substitutes H(Delta). It does not inherit Yin's white AP-CSDM MUSIC assumptions or reported performance.

For a modal pressure H(f,r,z)=sum_l b_l(f,z) exp(-i k_l(f)r)/sqrt(k_l(f)r), the autoproduct contains every (l,n) term b_l(f+) b_n*(f-) with different complex propagation phases. No same-index mode pairing is imposed between the two independently solved frequencies, no unstable mode-ID correspondence is used to discard cross terms, and no truth-guided path selection occurs. All modes returned under the inherited phase-speed interval remain in each pressure sum.
