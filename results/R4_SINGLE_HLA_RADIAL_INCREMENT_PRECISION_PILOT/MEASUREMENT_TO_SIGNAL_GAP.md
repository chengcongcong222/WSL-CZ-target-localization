# Conditional radial features → real single-HLA signal: unresolved gaps

This pilot injects six noisy 200 s net horizontal range-increment rates. It does not extract them from receiver signals. Correlation papers' local radial velocity, a peak's magnitude, and these net-window features are different quantities; their mapping is NOT_ESTABLISHED. Signed bearings, exact navigation, independent Gaussian feature errors and a calibrated zero point (except UB) remain hypotheses.

For p(f,t)=S(f,t)H(f,t), the cross-time product contains S(f,t)S*(f,t+tau). Eliminating a common source amplitude does not eliminate cross-time source PHASE. A bare propagation transfer H must not replace received complex pressure. Unknown emitted frequency must not be bypassed using true f0 demodulation or true-frequency conversion into q; no such input is used here. A fitted common b is a convenient zero-point proxy, not complete treatment of unknown spectrum/frequency/reference/phase.

Scalar analytic control: p(t)=A exp{i(omega-k*q)t}; changing q to q+delta and omega to omega+k*delta preserves p. Preflight verifies the identity without generating audio or complex receiver spectra. This is a source/motion confounding example in a simple component model, not a no-go theorem for all multimodal correlation methods. E1-G0's unknown-frequency/reference negative admission remains unchanged.

With illustrative c_ref=1500 m/s and T=200 s, ordinary spectral-grid velocity scales c_ref/(f*T) at201/235/283Hz are .0373134/.0319149/.0265018 m/s. They are not measured sigma_q, estimation lower bounds or certified resolutions; zero padding does not automatically create resolving information. The task's .02/.05/.10/.20 m/s are precision VALUE bins, not demonstrated extractable precisions.

Real extraction must separately address unknown source phase and frequency reference, 200 s coherence, window acceleration/sign crossings, multimodal phase velocity, slant vs horizontal range, weak received fields, peak ambiguity, zero-point drift, and correlated bearing/radial/multifrequency statistics from the same recording. Multiple frequencies are not automatically independent radial measurements. H1 source-amplitude handling has not certified any of these steps.

The existing Xu2018/Li2019 equations and provenance are read from the archived E1 measurement model lock, preserving their assumptions and historic negative Gates. A newer paper describes line-spectrum correlation/radial velocity followed by bearing-aided range estimation: [Wang et al., 2025, publisher DOI page](https://www.cpsjournals.cn/article/doi/10.12395/0371-0025.2024010). This turn retrieved its publisher HTML abstract/figure captions only, not a full estimator formula replication. Its reported experimental conditions cannot transfer to 50–60 km first-CZ geometry. No novelty is claimed for least squares, bearing/radial fusion or nuisance profiling; the pilot evaluates conditional value in this frozen geometry.

A positive result may justify a separately authorized extraction study targeting the useful parameter and measured precision bins. It cannot establish that the original HLA delivers the feature, resolve the unknown source-frequency problem, or award R4 progress. A negative/weak result pertains only to this observation definition, finite search and assumed noise—not every radial-information method.
